Note that \instname\ data are reduced by the ESPRESSO pipeline, i.e. \pipename.
Also, the  \href{https://www.eso.org/sci/software/pipelines/esotk/esotk-pipe-recipes.html}{eso-tk pipeline} has to be installed as well if one wants to combine the reduced invididual spectra with the task {\bf combine\_science}.

The \instname\ EDPS workflow supports also the reduction of data taken in POLARIMETRIC (both circular and linear polarization) and EGGS instrument modes. For POLARIMETRIC data, only extraction
of 1D spectra is supported, not the computation of Stokes parameters.

Observations of the Sun (either HARPS or HELIOS)  are currently not supported.

Graphical reports are currently unavailable.
